Title

Ascetic writer for your Mac and iPhone

Lists and tasks

Type a dash for a list, two brackets for a task. Return keeps the list going; click a box to tick it off.

Images

Paste a photo or a screenshot. It fits the page, and opens full size with a click.

Links

Links look like the words around them. Hold ⌘ and click to open one.

Folders

Group notes when you need to. Make a folder from +, or right-click a note and move it.

Move to a new folder

Hold a note and file it into a new folder in one step.

Just the page

One click, or ⌃⌘S, and the list slides away. Only the writing is left.

Themes

Dark by default. Dark Sepia, Midnight and a paper-white Light when you want them.

On iPhone and iPad

The same quiet page everywhere, synced through your own iCloud.
